# Team Images

This folder contains images used in the SMARTMEDI website.

## Images Needed:

### team.jpg
- **Location**: `/assets/images/team.jpg`
- **Purpose**: Team showcase section on the homepage
- **Recommended Size**: 
  - Width: 600px - 800px
  - Height: 400px - 600px
  - Aspect Ratio: 4:3 or 16:10 (landscape)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Description**: Professional photo of medical team/doctors in clinical attire

## How to Add:

1. Prepare your team photo (professional healthcare workers in clinical uniforms)
2. Save it as `team.jpg` in this directory
3. The image will automatically appear on the homepage in the "Meet Our Expert Medical Team" section

## Notes:

- The image will be responsive and adjust to different screen sizes
- It includes a subtle animation on page load
- Professional lighting and high-quality images work best
- Ensure at least 300px width for mobile viewing
