Frequently Asked Questions

What equipment do I need for video consultation?

You just need a smartphone, tablet, or computer with a stable internet connection and a camera. We recommend having a quiet, well-lit space for the consultation.

Is my privacy protected during video consultation?

Yes, absolutely. All video consultations are encrypted with military-grade security. We comply with HIPAA and GDPR standards. Your privacy and medical information are completely confidential.

Can I get a prescription from video consultation?

Yes, doctors can issue valid digital prescriptions after the consultation. These can be directly sent to pharmacies or downloaded for your records.

What if I have internet connection issues?

If you experience connection issues, you can reschedule or request a callback. Our support team will help you troubleshoot. You won't be charged if the issue was on our end.

Are video consultations covered by insurance?

Most major insurance providers cover video consultations. We handle the claim processing. Check with your insurance provider or contact our support team for specific details.

Can I reschedule or cancel my consultation?

Yes, you can reschedule or cancel up to 30 minutes before the consultation with no charges. Cancellations within 30 minutes may incur a nominal fee.
