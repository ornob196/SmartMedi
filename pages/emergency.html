Important First Aid Tips

Cardiac Arrest

  1. Call ambulance immediately
  2. Start CPR (chest compression)
  3. Continue until ambulance arrives
  4. Use AED if available

Severe Bleeding

  1. Apply direct pressure with clean cloth
  2. Elevate the affected area
  3. Apply tourniquet if necessary
  4. Call ambulance immediately

Stroke Signs (FAST)

  1. Face - Check for facial drooping
  2. Arm - Check arm weakness
  3. Speech - Check for slurred speech
  4. Time - Call ambulance immediately

Fractures

  1. Immobilize the injured area
  2. Apply ice to reduce swelling
  3. Elevate if possible
  4. Seek medical attention

Burn Injuries

  1. Cool the burn with water (10-20 min)
  2. Remove jewelry/tight clothing
  3. Cover with clean, dry cloth
  4. Seek medical help for severe burns

Eye Injuries

  1. Don't rub the eye
  2. Rinse with clean water gently
  3. Cover with sterile pad
  4. Seek immediate medical care
