Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about SMARTMEDI

How do I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment by visiting our website, selecting a doctor, choosing a date and time, and filling in your details. You'll receive a confirmation via email and SMS.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets. We also support cashless payment through insurance partners.
Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?
Yes, you can cancel or reschedule your appointment up to 24 hours before the scheduled time from your patient portal or by contacting our support team.
Is my personal information secure?
Yes, we use industry-leading encryption and security measures to protect your personal and medical information. All data is stored securely and complies with healthcare privacy regulations.
How does telemedicine consultation work?
For video consultations, you'll receive a secure link before your appointment. Simply click the link at the scheduled time to connect with your doctor via video call. Ensure you have a stable internet connection and webcam.
Can I download my medical records?
Yes, you can download your medical records, prescriptions, and lab reports in PDF format from your patient portal. These records are securely stored and accessible anytime.
